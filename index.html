What leaves your browser

  • An address: nothing. It's answered on this page. Your seed: never asked for, and refused if pasted.
  • A transaction: only the transaction ID, to api.blockchair.com, and to THORChain's records (gateway.liquify.com, or midgard.ninerealms.com) if it's a THORChain swap.
  • Only if you press "Check bridges and swaps": its public output addresses, to mainnet.api.bridge.nearone.org. If one is an Omni deposit, the page also reads NEAR's Zcash light client from rpc.mainnet.fastnear.com, sending nothing about you or the transaction, to work out the refund deadline.
  • Those services see your IP address, like any website. Nothing is stored: no accounts, cookies or analytics.
